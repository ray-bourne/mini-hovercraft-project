# Mini Hovercraft — Horizontal Propulsion Model

**ENGR110: Engineering Orientation & Ethics | Northern Caribbean University | April 2022**

A small-scale, hand-sized hovercraft designed and built from scratch, demonstrating air-cushion lift and horizontal thrust propulsion engineered end-to-end from concept sketches through a formal design comparison to a working physical prototype.

## Project Goal
Design and build a working hovercraft model capable of propelling itself horizontally across a distance of 4+ yards, using a single propulsion system to generate both lift (air cushion) and thrust (forward movement), built to a **$50 budget** and simple, accessible materials.

## Engineering Design Process
Two candidate designs were drafted in **AutoCAD**, then evaluated against a formal weighted criteria matrix before selecting a final design: a structured engineering decision process rather than just building the first idea:

| Criteria | Max Score | Design 1 | Design 2 (Selected) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost Effectiveness | 40 | 25 | 30 |
| Weight | 20 | 10 | 10 |
| Travel Distance (4-6ft) | 20 | 10 | 15 |
| Simplicity | 10 | 10 | 5 |
| Power Consumed | 10 | 8 | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** | **63** | **70** |

**Design 2** (single fan blade, four-battery control panel) was selected — it traded off slightly higher power consumption and build complexity for significantly better cost-effectiveness and travel distance versus the three-fan Design 1.

## Build
- **Skirt:** Dual-layer plastic air cushion, cut and taped to hold air pressure from the propeller downdraft
- **Propulsion:** Single DC motor + propeller, mounted on a secured motor stand
- **Power:** 4-battery compartment with a secured cover (testing showed the cover itself measurably improved performance by preventing battery displacement during operation)
- **Base:** Black cardboard base panel with a plastic duct template

## Results & Troubleshooting
- Confirmed working horizontal propulsion, meeting the 4+ yard travel objective
- Diagnosed and resolved a low-battery-charge issue that was initially causing slow movement
- Empirically tested and confirmed that running with the battery compartment cover **on** improved performance over running with it off (prevented battery movement during operation)
- Noted a ~15-20 minute battery life under continuous operation

## Engineering Skills Demonstrated
- CAD design (AutoCAD, Autodesk Fusion 360)
- Structured design comparison using a weighted evaluation matrix
- Physical prototyping and assembly
- Basic wiring and electrical assembly (motor, battery compartment, terminals)
- Empirical testing and iterative troubleshooting
